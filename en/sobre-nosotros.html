ABOUT US

We built Comando because ads get reviewed once a month.

We are a marketing and product team from Latin America. We have spent years managing ad spend for businesses and agencies, and we kept hitting the same wall: the data lives in Ads Manager, the decisions happen in a weekly meeting and, in between, the budget keeps running.

What Comando is

Comando is a team of AI agents that reviews your Meta and TikTok ads every day and answers you in a chat. You ask it like you would a person: which campaign spends without bringing results, why the cost per lead went up, which ad is burned out. It answers with your numbers and tells you what to change.

When something needs changing, it proposes it and waits for your answer. Nothing runs in your campaigns unless you confirm it.

What we believe

  • Decide with the numbers in front of you. A number without context is useless: every answer says what happened, why and what to do.
  • You are in control. Comando recommends and executes, but only after your confirmation, and it keeps a record of every change.
  • Sales decide, not forms. A lead is not a sale. We match the real sales in your CRM with your campaigns to know what actually works.
  • Fixed price. We do not charge a percentage of your spend: growing should not cost you more for the same work.
  • Your data is yours. We only process what the service needs, and you can ask us to delete it.

How we work

We build with the people who use it. Each agent comes from a concrete problem a customer brought us, gets tested on real accounts and is fixed with what happens day to day, not with what we assume on a whiteboard.

We are a small team. That means when you write, you get an answer from someone who knows the product.

Where we are

Made in Latin America, for Latin America. We support Spanish, Portuguese and English, and we work with the prices and the realities of the region: small and mid-sized ad accounts, sales teams that sell on WhatsApp and budgets with no room for waste.
